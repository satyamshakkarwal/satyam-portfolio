My story

I started building stores because I was tired of sites that looked nice but didn't sell. So I learned to handle both sides: design and development.

Over the last 3.5+ years I've built and managed 15+ Shopify stores and Framer websites for eCommerce and service brands across fashion, healthcare, lifestyle and more. From the first wireframe to launch, I handle the design, the build, and the small details that make a site feel premium.

My focus is simple: stores and sites that look great, load fast, and turn visitors into customers. I design and build everything myself, so it stays clean and consistent from start to finish.

Today I work mostly on premium Shopify and Framer builds for brands that want to stand out and sell more. I'm currently open to new projects.
